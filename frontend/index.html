EXPO PROJECT • RESEARCH ANALYSIS

From satellite signal to an executable mining decision

ASTROVA is an Earth-observation decision-support prototype. It combines a location, mineral profile, spectral indicators, weather context, and operational assumptions so an exploration team can explain why a site is prioritized and how that finding becomes a five-day work plan.

01
Site and mineral selection

The user selects Manganese, Nickel, or Cobalt and chooses a location by coordinates or map click. This establishes the analysis area.

02
Earth-observation feature preparation

The model uses blue, green, red, near-infrared, SWIR, NDVI, and NDWI-style inputs. These describe surface reflectance, vegetation, and moisture patterns relevant to a screening exercise.

03
AI prospectivity scoring

A Random Forest classifier evaluates the selected location against the mineral profile and produces a prospectivity percentage: high (70%+), medium (40–69.9%), or low (<40%).

04
Operations and weather check

Current conditions and a five-day weather forecast identify rain, wind, heat, and visibility constraints that may affect safe extraction, haulage, and road access.

05
Production, risk, and resources

The prospectivity scenario drives a five-day production proposal, readiness level, operational-risk score, workforce, truck, and fuel recommendations.

06
Decision and next action

Present the map, score, weather plan, resource plan, and risk together. A real deployment would send high-priority regions for geological validation and sampling.

What would make the recommendation more useful

Validated Earth data

Atmospherically corrected Sentinel-2 imagery, cloud masks, historical scenes, and mineral occurrence labels to replace the prototype feature simulation.

Geology and terrain

Lithology, faults, soil/alteration layers, DEM slope, drainage, road access, and lease boundaries to make site suitability spatially realistic.

Mine operations

Equipment availability, maintenance status, operator rosters, operating hours, haul-cycle time, stockpile capacity, and production targets.

Field and safety data

Assay samples, drilling logs, ground-truth points, road condition, water availability, dust exposure, and environmental restrictions.

Prototype scope: location-dependent spectral inputs are simulated for the expo. Sentinel-2 has visible, near-infrared, and SWIR bands that can be used in a future validated pipeline; field sampling remains essential before decisions are made.
